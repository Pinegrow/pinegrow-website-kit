Classes and timetable

Find your class, keep your mat.

Four group classes, eighteen times a week, plus private sessions. Every class stops taking bookings at twelve.

The classes

Five ways to practise

All classes are taught in Slovenian and English. Mats, blocks, bolsters and blankets are here for you.

01

Vinyasa

75 min · €18 · Mats 01-12

A steady, breath-led flow that builds heat and strength. Expect sun salutations, standing sequences and a long rest at the end.

All levels with some yoga experience

02

Slow flow

60 or 75 min · €18 · Mats 01-12

The same shapes as vinyasa at half the pace, with time to notice how each one feels. A good first class.

Beginners welcome

03

Yin

90 min · €20 · Mats 01-12

Long, supported floor poses held for three to five minutes, with bolsters and blankets. Quiet, slow and deeper than it looks.

Beginners welcome

04

Prenatal

60 min · €16 · Mats 01-10

Gentle movement, breathing and rest for every trimester, taught by a teacher trained in prenatal yoga. Tea after class.

From the 14th week, with your doctor's OK

05

Private session

60 min · €65 · By appointment

One to one or with a partner, at the studio or at your home in Ljubljana. For injuries, first steps or a practice of your own.

Any level

Your first class

What happens when you come

Ask us anything first →
  1. 01

    Book a mat

    Write or call and tell us which class. We hold your mat until five minutes before the start.

  2. 02

    Come fifteen minutes early

    There are changing rooms and a shelf for shoes and phones. Tell the teacher about injuries or a pregnancy.

  3. 03

    Practise at your pace

    With twelve people in the room the teacher offers you an easier or a stronger version of every pose.

  4. 04

    Stay for tea

    There is always a pot on after class. Your first class is €12, whichever one you choose.
