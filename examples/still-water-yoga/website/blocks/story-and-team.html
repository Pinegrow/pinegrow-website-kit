Maja in the studio, spring 2025

How it started

I wanted a class where the teacher knows your name.

After ten years of teaching in gyms with forty people to a room, I started to recognise backs better than faces. In 2019 I found an old bookbinder's workshop on the Trnovo embankment with room for exactly twelve mats, and that became the rule.

Today four of us teach here. We plan our classes together, talk about the people who come, and keep the room the way it was on the first day: wooden floor, river light, no mirrors.

Maja Kos, founder

First class (steps)

Your first class

What happens when you come

Ask us anything first →
  1. 01

    Book a mat

    Write or call and tell us which class. We hold your mat until five minutes before the start.

  2. 02

    Come fifteen minutes early

    There are changing rooms and a shelf for shoes and phones. Tell the teacher about injuries or a pregnancy.

  3. 03

    Practise at your pace

    With twelve people in the room the teacher offers you an easier or a stronger version of every pose.

  4. 04

    Stay for tea

    There is always a pot on after class. Your first class is €12, whichever one you choose.
