Twelve is how many mats fit in our room with space to stretch your arms, and how many people one teacher can really watch. It means your teacher sees your knee drifting in, remembers that your shoulder was sore last week and has time to answer your question after class.

When a class is full, it is full. Join the waiting list and we'll write when a mat opens.

Yin by lamplight

Two evenings a week we turn off the ceiling lights and teach yin by the lamps along the wall. Ninety minutes, six or seven long poses, and nothing to achieve.

Bring warm socks. We have the rest.
