How it started

I wanted a class where the teacher knows your name.

After ten years of teaching in gyms with forty people to a room, I started to recognise backs better than faces. In 2019 I found an old bookbinder's workshop on the Trnovo embankment with room for exactly twelve mats, and that became the rule.

Today four of us teach here. We plan our classes together, talk about the people who come, and keep the room the way it was on the first day: wooden floor, river light, no mirrors.

Maja Kos, founder
