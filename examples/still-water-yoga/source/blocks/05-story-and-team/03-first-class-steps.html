Your first class

What happens when you come

Ask us anything first →
  1. 01

    Book a mat

    Write or call and tell us which class. We hold your mat until five minutes before the start.

  2. 02

    Come fifteen minutes early

    There are changing rooms and a shelf for shoes and phones. Tell the teacher about injuries or a pregnancy.

  3. 03

    Practise at your pace

    With twelve people in the room the teacher offers you an easier or a stronger version of every pose.

  4. 04

    Stay for tea

    There is always a pot on after class. Your first class is €12, whichever one you choose.
