Before you come

Questions people ask

I have never done yoga. Which class should I take?+

Slow flow or yin. Both are taught for beginners, and with twelve people the teacher will show you every pose. Tell us it is your first class when you book.

What do I need to bring?+

Comfortable clothes and a bottle of water. Mats, blocks, straps, bolsters and blankets are at the studio and are cleaned after every class.

What if the class is full?+

Ask to go on the waiting list. When someone cancels we write to the first person on the list.

How late can I cancel?+

Up to six hours before the class. Later than that, the class is counted from your card, because the mat stayed empty.

When can I start prenatal yoga?+

From the 14th week of pregnancy, once your doctor or midwife says it is fine. You can keep coming until the week you give birth.
