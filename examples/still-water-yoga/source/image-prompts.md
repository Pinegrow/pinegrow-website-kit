# Image prompts

One photographic style for every image: documentary photography on a 35 mm lens, soft natural daylight from tall
windows (warm lamps for the evening images), a real studio with a pale oak floor, white walls and plain grey-green
mats, muted natural colors, real people of different ages and body types in ordinary practice clothes, calm but not
posed. No sunsets, beaches, lotus flowers, candles in rows, silhouettes, pastel washes, logos or text in the image.

Save each as JPG in `website/images/` with the file name below (it replaces the grey placeholder).
