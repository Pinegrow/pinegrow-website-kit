# Splash Swim School kit brief

Purpose: A website kit for one independent swim school in Bristol
Businesses and pages: Splash Swim School. Home, Lessons, Prices and timetable, About us, Contact
CSS: Tailwind CSS
WordPress: no
Designs: 3
Block library: yes
Publishing: Static site with Netlify-ready enquiries
Images: Generated

## Concept

Borrow the clarity of a pool timetable. Give each swimmer a clear route through the site, and keep dates, times and prices easy to compare. Pair the structured boards with candid photographs taken at water level and around the pool. Use rounded display type and a broad accent stripe to give the kit a friendly poolside character.

Open Home with “Find your feet in the water.” Give parents and adult learners three direct routes to the lesson that fits them. Write to adults as learners in their own right and use practical language about what happens at the first lesson.

| Page | What visitors can do |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Choose baby and parent, kids’ or adult lessons and see what a Splash lesson feels like. |
| Lessons | Compare the three lesson groups, see what to expect and find the right starting point. |
| Prices and timetable | Compare example fees and weekly sessions, then ask about a place. |
| About us | Learn how Splash teaches, how teachers place learners and what support is available. |
| Contact | Ask about lessons, pool access or a private booking and find out how venue details are shared. |

## Build and content

Retain the 18 Crux block structures, the categorised Insert library, Content panel annotations, shared header and footer components, and the blank starter. Adapt every block to the swim school, including the supplementary pass, gift voucher and private lesson enquiry blocks.

Bristol is the supplied location. The fees, age groups, course lengths and times are sample content. Confirm these and the venue, access details and enquiry destination before launch. Use generated pool photos throughout and avoid implying that the pictured people are Splash staff or learners.
