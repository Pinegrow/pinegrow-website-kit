About Splash

Meet Splash Swim School.

We teach babies, children and adults in Bristol. We make space for first-timers, nervous swimmers and anyone coming back to the pool.

How we teach ↓ Explore lessons ↗

How we teach

We start with what a swimmer can do comfortably. Teachers demonstrate one skill at a time, give learners room to try it and offer specific feedback before moving on.

See what to expect ↗

What progress looks like

We set the next goal with the swimmer or their parent, then make time to practise it in the pool.

  1. 01

    Choose a starting group

    Tell us how much time the swimmer has spent in the water and what they can comfortably do.

  2. 02

    Practise one skill at a time

    That might mean blowing bubbles, floating with support or practising a calmer breath between strokes.

  3. 03

    Review what comes next

    We’ll talk through what’s improved and what to work on next, including when it’s time to try a different group.
