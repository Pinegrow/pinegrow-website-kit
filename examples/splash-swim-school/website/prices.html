Before you book

Do I need to book in advance? +

Yes. Use the contact form to ask about a place. We’ll confirm the group, dates and fee with you before booking.

Can I join partway through a course? +

Ask about the group you’d like to join. We’ll let you know whether it has a space or when the next course starts.

What happens if I miss a lesson? +

Ask about the current missed-lesson policy when you book, so you know what to do if your plans change.
