Questions and answers

Before your first swim

Can I join if I’m nervous in the water? +

Yes. Tell us when you enquire. We can talk through what the first lesson will involve and help you start where you’re comfortable.

Do I get in the pool with my baby? +

Yes. One parent or carer gets in with the baby. Bring swimwear for yourself and a swim nappy for your baby.

What if I’m unsure which level to choose? +

Tell us about the swimmer’s experience, including anything they can already do comfortably. We’ll help you choose a group.
