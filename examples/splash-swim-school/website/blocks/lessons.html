Three steps

Your first lesson

A little preparation helps everyone settle in. Here’s what to expect at the pool.

  1. 01

    Pack your swim kit

    Bring swimwear and a towel. If you’re joining a baby class, pack a swim nappy and swimwear for the adult getting in.

  2. 02

    Meet your teacher

    Check your confirmation for arrival and changing details. Tell the teacher how you feel in the water and what you’d like help with.

  3. 03

    Start at your level

    Your teacher will explain the pool routine and give you an activity that suits your experience. You can take a pause whenever you need one.
