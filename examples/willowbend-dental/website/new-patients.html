New patients

Make yourself at home.

Here’s how to prepare for your first appointment, what happens during the visit and who to ask if you have questions.

Before you arrive

What to bring

Call ahead if you’d like help transferring dental records, checking insurance benefits or arranging an accessible visit.

  • Photo ID and insurance card, if you have one
  • A list of your medications and allergies
  • Recent dental records or X-rays, if available
  • Any questions you want to ask your dentist

New patients

Your first visit, step by step.

  1. 01

    Get settled.

    We’ll review your paperwork and ask about past dental visits and anything you’d like us to know.

  2. 02

    Meet your dentist.

    We’ll explain the exam, talk through your questions and discuss whether X-rays are needed.

  3. 03

    Make a plan together.

    We’ll review what we found, explain your options and help you decide what to schedule next.

Planning your visit

Common questions

Can I schedule visits for more than one family member?+

Yes. Ask the front desk about appointments close together when you call. We’ll check what’s available for each family member.

Can you check my insurance before my visit?+

Call the office with your plan information. We can help you check benefits and explain how we prepare a cost estimate.

What if I’m nervous about the appointment?+

Tell us when you schedule or when you arrive. We can talk through what will happen, build in breaks and agree on how you’ll let us know when you want to pause.

Ready to come in?

Request a visit and our front desk will help you find a time that works for your family.
