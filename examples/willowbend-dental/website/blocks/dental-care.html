Dental care details

How we can help

Checkups & cleanings

We review your dental history, check your teeth and gums, and discuss whether a cleaning or X-rays are appropriate for that visit.

Ask about routine exams, professional cleanings and help with brushing or flossing at home.

Children’s dentistry

Children can meet the team, see how the chair works and ask questions before the exam. We adapt the visit to their age and comfort.

Ask about first checkups, brushing routines, sealants and planning family appointments.

Restorative care

If a tooth is chipped, worn or damaged by decay, we’ll examine it and explain the treatment options, expected visits and costs.

Ask about tooth-colored fillings, crowns and follow-up appointments.
