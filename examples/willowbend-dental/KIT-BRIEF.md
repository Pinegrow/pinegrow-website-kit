# Kit brief

Purpose: A reusable single-practice starter with the fictional Willowbend Family Dental as the worked example.
Businesses and pages: Family dental practice. Home, Our practice, Dental care, New patients, Visit & contact, plus a blank starter.
CSS: Tailwind CSS 4
WordPress: no
Designs: 3
Block library: yes
Publishing: Static hosting. Appointment requests are set up for a practice-approved form service, with Netlify support available.
Images: Generated editorial photographs, replaceable with real practice photos.

## Concept

The site follows a first visit. The home page pairs a warm arrival photograph with a clear appointment action, then uses a chairside-style care index and a three-step visit guide. Thin rules organize hours, services and common questions in the same way throughout the kit.

## Color roles

Page is the main background and surface is the soft inset. Ink and muted separate headlines from supporting copy. Line is used for rules. Primary and on-primary are the accent pair, and band and on-band are the closing section pair.

## Fonts

Sans is for reading and navigation, display is for headlines and care titles, and mono is for short index labels and hours.

## Saved designs

- Willow uses warm ivory, moss and olive with DM Sans and Manrope.
- Harbor uses cool white, teal and deep marine with Instrument Sans and Lora.
- Dusk uses charcoal plum, rose and ivory with Outfit and Playfair Display.

## Notes

Practice details and imagery are sample content. The request form collects contact and scheduling preferences only. Replace sample details and connect the form to the practice’s approved workflow before publishing.
