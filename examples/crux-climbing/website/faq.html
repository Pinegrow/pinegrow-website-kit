Your first visit

What is bouldering? Do I need experience? +

Bouldering means climbing short routes above padded mats without ropes. If you’re new, ask the team to point out an easy route close to the mat.

What happens when I arrive? +

Tell the team at the desk that it’s your first visit. They’ll go through the safety briefing, help you with shoes if you need them and show you where to start.

What should I wear and bring? +

Wear clothes you can move in, bring a water bottle and tie back long hair. Bring climbing shoes if you have them, or bring socks and ask about shoe hire at the desk.

Do I need to book ahead? +

Check with the team before coming, especially for coaching or a group visit.

Bringing children

How old do children need to be? +

Tell us the children’s ages and the session you’re interested in. The team can confirm the minimum age for family climbing, coaching or a party.

Does an adult need to stay? +

Check before you book. The team can confirm who needs to stay and how many children each adult can supervise for your session.

Can my child try climbing with a coach? +

Ask about a coached session if your child would like help getting started. Tell us their age, whether they’ve climbed before and any support they might need.

What do I need to know before booking a party? +

Send us your preferred dates, roughly how many children are coming and their ages. Ask what’s available and which adults need to stay.
