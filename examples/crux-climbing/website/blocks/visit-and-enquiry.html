Questions and answers

First-time questions

Do I need to be strong? +

No. Start with an easy wall and take breaks when you want. Your legs do more of the work than you might expect.

What should I wear? +

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Bring socks if you plan to hire shoes, and tie long hair back.

Do I need to book? +

Ask the team about first visits and coached sessions before you come, especially if you’re bringing a group.
