Before you pay

Can I try one visit first? +

Yes. Single entry is the way to start if you’re deciding whether you want to climb more often.

Can I hire shoes each time? +

Ask at the desk for your size. Check whether you’ll need to pay for hire separately if you choose a pass or membership.

Can I pause or cancel a membership? +

Ask about notice periods, pauses and cancellations before choosing a plan.
