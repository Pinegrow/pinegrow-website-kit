Before you bring the kids

What age can children start? +

Tell us the children’s age range when you enquire. We’ll confirm the minimum age for the session or party you’re looking at.

Do adults need to stay? +

Ask us about supervision when you book. We’ll confirm who needs to stay and how many children each adult can supervise.

What should we bring? +

Comfortable clothes, socks if hiring shoes, and a water bottle. Tell us if anyone in the group needs extra support.

Let’s plan a party.

Send your preferred dates, an estimated number of children and their age range. We’ll get back to you about what’s available.
