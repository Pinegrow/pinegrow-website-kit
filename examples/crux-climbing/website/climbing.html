Your first three moves

New to bouldering? Here’s what to do when you arrive.

  1. 01

    Check in

    Tell the team it’s your first time. They’ll take you through the safety briefing and show you where to start.

  2. 02

    Get your shoes

    Wear clothes you can move in. Bring climbing shoes if you have them or ask for a pair at the desk.

  3. 03

    Find an easy climb

    Pick something close to the mat and take your time. Ask the team to point out a good first climb.

First-time questions

Do I need to be strong? +

No. Start with an easy wall and take breaks when you want. Your legs do more of the work than you might expect.

What should I wear? +

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Bring socks if you plan to hire shoes, and tie long hair back.

Do I need to book? +

Ask the team about first visits and coached sessions before you come, especially if you’re bringing a group.
