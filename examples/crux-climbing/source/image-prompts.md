# Crux Climbing photo brief

**Shared direction.** Candid UK independent gym editorial photography, in the same converted Leeds warehouse. Soft daylight from industrial windows, dark slate and honey plywood walls with vivid chartreuse, coral and blue climbing holds, chalk dust caught in the light, padded charcoal floor, subtle analogue grain and natural skin tones. Believable bouldering technique and anatomy. No ropes, harnesses, text, signage, branding or watermarks. Keep the subject clear in a centred crop and allow room at the edges.
