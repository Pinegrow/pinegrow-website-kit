# Kit brief

Purpose: A website kit for one client, Crux Climbing, a bouldering gym in Leeds
Businesses and pages: Crux Climbing. Home, Climbing and classes, Prices and memberships, Kids and parties, Visit us, Frequently asked questions
CSS: Tailwind CSS
WordPress: no
Designs: 3
Block library: yes
Publishing: Netlify
Images: client-supplied and generated

## Concept

**The Crux session board.** Borrow the look of the board you check when you walk into the gym. Use clear rules, short labels and bold prices you can find at a glance. Pair that structure with wide, candid photos of people on the wall. Keep chalk texture in the photography and give practical information a clean background.

Open with **“Make your first move.”** Put **“Plan your first visit”** beside a photo of someone reaching for a hold. Give families and regular climbers direct links to kids’ sessions, parties and memberships just below. Explain bouldering in one line: “Climb short walls above padded mats, without ropes.”

| Page | What visitors can do |
| --- | --- |
| Home | See what it feels like to climb at Crux and pick where to go next. |
| Climbing and classes | See how a first visit works and compare beginner sessions and coaching. |
| Prices and memberships | Scan separate boards for entry, equipment hire and memberships, with the price, billing period and inclusions together. |
| Kids and parties | Find age guidance and supervision details, see what happens at a party and send an enquiry. |
| Visit us | Check opening hours, travel options, accessibility and what to bring, or send a question. |
| Frequently asked questions | Find answers about first visits, shoe hire, children’s sessions and supervision. |

Use climbing action in the Home hero, a coached session for Climbing and classes, family climbing on Kids and parties, and a view of reception on Visit us. Keep faces and climbing action in frame on desktop and mobile. The three photos supplied by Crux show an evening session on Home, family climbing on Kids and parties, and reception on Visit us. See the [image credits](source/image-credits.md) for all placements.

Use heavy, oversized display type for short, playful headlines. A tilted colour strip highlights the second line of the photo heroes. Use the sans for reading and the mono for hours, prices and practical labels.

## Saved designs

- **Chalk** is the default. Warm white, charcoal and a sharp yellow accent, with tall condensed headlines and rough detail in the photos.
- **Night** uses near-black backgrounds, coral accents and wide, heavy headlines for an evening-session feel.
- **Tape** uses pale lilac, saturated blue and rounded headline forms for a brighter gym-poster look.

## Notes

- Six starter pages and a blank starter. The drag-and-drop library will include shared navigation and footer, photo and text heroes, first-visit steps, class listings, price and membership tables, family sections, opening hours, gift vouchers, and an enquiry form. Use Pinegrow components for the shared navigation and footer and Content panel fields for text and images.
- Prepare Netlify Forms markup for enquiries. The client will publish the project from the `website` folder when ready.
- Confirm the gym’s prices, hours, address, class schedule, age and supervision rules, and accessibility details before publishing. Mark sample information clearly in the kit wherever those details are needed for the layout.
- The client approved the concept and requested bolder, more playful headlines. The client will open the project in Pinegrow.
