What happens at your first appointment

Wear comfortable clothes. Everything else we’ll sort out together.

  1. 1

    We listen

    What happened, what hurts and what you want to get back to.

  2. 2

    We assess

  3. 3

    We treat

    Hands-on treatment in the same session, so you leave feeling better.

  4. 4

    You get a plan

    A few exercises on your phone and a date to check progress.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need a referral?

No. You can book directly. If you’re claiming on insurance, check whether your insurer needs one.

Which insurers do you work with?

We bill Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality and Aviva directly. Others can claim back with our receipt.

What should I wear?

Something comfortable you can move in. Shorts for knee problems, a vest top for shoulders.

Can I cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Book your first visit this week.

Online booking takes a minute. Or call us and we’ll find a time that works.
