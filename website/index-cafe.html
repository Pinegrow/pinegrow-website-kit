Our story

We bake everything in the room behind the counter.

Ember & Oat started with one oven and a sourdough starter called Morag. Five years later we still bake every loaf, bun and tart ourselves, a few metres from where you sit.

Our coffee comes from Steampunk Roasters, two streets away. The milk is from a farm in East Lothian. The oats, of course, are Scottish.
