Process · time line

Sheet A-03

How a room plan runs

From the first visit to a plan your builder can follow, in about three weeks.

  1. Day 1

    Measure

    We visit, listen, measure every wall and photograph the light.

  2. Week 1

    Two directions

    Two layouts with samples you can touch and a budget for each.

  3. Week 2

    Detail

    Drawings, lighting and a shopping list with every product linked.

  4. Week 3

    Hand over

    We walk you and your builder through the plan, on site.

Questions · FAQ

Sheet A-06

General notes

Do you work outside Portland?

Yes. For projects more than an hour away we plan site visits around the key moments and work online in between.

Can we keep some of our furniture?

Usually most of it. We measure what you love first and design around it.

Do you work with our builder?

Gladly. Our drawings are made for builders. We can also recommend builders we trust and compare quotes with you.

What does a site visit cost?

A site consultation is €240. If you go on to a room plan, we take it off the fee.
