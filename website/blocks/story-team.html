Story · text and image

Sheet B-01

We start with how you live, not with a mood board.

Before we draw anything, we spend time in your space. Where does the morning light fall? Where do the bags land after school? Which chair does everyone fight over?

Then we measure everything, twice. Good drawings are the reason our projects finish on time: builders know exactly what to do, and you know exactly what it costs.

Process · time line

Sheet A-03

How a room plan runs

From the first visit to a plan your builder can follow, in about three weeks.

  1. Day 1

    Measure

    We visit, listen, measure every wall and photograph the light.

  2. Week 1

    Two directions

    Two layouts with samples you can touch and a budget for each.

  3. Week 2

    Detail

    Drawings, lighting and a shopping list with every product linked.

  4. Week 3

    Hand over

    We walk you and your builder through the plan, on site.

Process · numbered steps

What happens at your first appointment

Wear comfortable clothes. Everything else we’ll sort out together.

  1. 1

    We listen

    What happened, what hurts and what you want to get back to.

  2. 2

    Movement, strength and a clear explanation of what’s going on.

  3. 3

    We treat

    Hands-on treatment in the same session, so you leave feeling better.

  4. 4

    You get a plan

    A few exercises on your phone and a date to check progress.
