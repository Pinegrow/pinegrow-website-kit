Five small changes that make a living room calmer

Nov 12, 2026 · Ingrid Holm

Before you buy anything new, try moving what you already have. Most rooms feel restless because there is no clear place for the eye to rest.

1. Choose one quiet wall

Keep one wall almost empty. A single picture or a plant is enough. It gives everything else room to breathe.

2. Lower the lights

Replace one ceiling light with two or three lamps at different heights. Evenings will feel completely different.

3. Hide the cables

It sounds small, but tidy cables make a room look finished. A box behind the sofa does wonders.

Good rooms are not full rooms. They are rooms where everything has a reason to be there.

Try one change a week and notice how the room feels. When you are ready for a bigger step, we are happy to help.
